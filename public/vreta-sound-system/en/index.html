03 · Sound

Slow pulse, long echoes

The signature sound is slow-pulse dub reggae, sung by a woman and a man, with sparse lines, deep bass and long echoes. The base tempo is 62 BPM. Each song then finds its own tempo.

Voices
A sparse, Hindustani-coloured female voice and a deep Jamaican male voice. The chorus opens into full voice.
Rhythm
Heavy, deep bass, spacious drums and tabla.
Texture
Echoing sitar, didgeridoo as a drone and harmonium chords.
Form
Mantra and chant, sometimes call and response, with a spacious dub bridge.
Variants
The same song can come acoustic with Ek & Lin or as techno trance with Leonid.
